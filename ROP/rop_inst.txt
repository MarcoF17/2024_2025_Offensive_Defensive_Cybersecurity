Inspect the memory of a process: sudo cat /proc/PID/maps

Libc change with patchelf:
    patchelf --set-interpreter PATH_TO_LOADER ELF_FILE
    patchelf --print-needed ELF_FILE
    patchelf --replace-needed LIBRARY_TO_BE_REPLACE NEW_LIBRARY ELF_FILE

To check if something is changed: readelf -s ELF_FILE | grep main
